## 2. The Problem: Why Mobile-Only Budgeting Feels Broken

### Frustration 1: The Tiny Screen Trap
Most budgeting tools force you onto a 6-inch phone display. When month-end arrives and you need to audit 150 transactions across four accounts, squinting at micro-buttons and endless modal taps is painful. You deserve a proper desktop screen with keyboard shortcuts and a full-width ledger.

### Frustration 2: Fragile Cloud Sync & Forced Bank Linking
Modern finance apps increasingly demand your bank logins through third-party data brokers, or rely on buggy cloud servers that silently overwrite local entries after app updates. If your internet drops or an API fails, you are locked out of your own financial ledger.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

### Does VaultPace connect to my bank account?
No. VaultPace is intentionally built without Plaid, MX, or direct bank screen-scraping. You maintain total privacy by entering transactions directly or importing standard CSV statements from your financial institution.

### Does VaultPace work when I am offline?
Yes. VaultPace operates client-side. All accounts, categories, budgets, and transactions are stored locally on your device. You can log expenses on an airplane or subway without an internet connection.

### Can I export my data at any time?
Yes. You own your data. Go to Settings > Data Management and click **Export Complete Ledger**. You will instantly download a clean, RFC-4180 compliant CSV file and an uncompressed JSON snapshot.

### What makes the "Today" budget marker special?
Standard budgeting apps only show a progress bar indicating how much money you have spent. VaultPace adds a vertical indicator line representing exactly where you should be today based on the number of days elapsed in the budget period. If your bar is behind the line, you are under pace; if it surpasses the line, you know to curb spending immediately.

### Are there any ads or data tracking?
None. No Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel, no tracking cookies, and no ad banners.
